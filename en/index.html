The idea

You. Me. And.

You and Me are the foundation: two people who chose each other. And is everything that can exist beyond that.

Relationship

Shape a relationship of your own

Exploring a more open relationship takes communication, trust and clear boundaries. We believe in taking it step by step, with care for yourself and each other.

We welcome everyone, whatever your relationship structure or life situation. Our advice is rooted in our own experiences, which are our starting point, but every relationship is its own.

Life

Create a life on your terms

We left Sweden and moved our whole family to a paradise island to make more room for the life we want to live. That journey has given us new perspectives and more time for what we care about: supporting others as they explore their possibilities and shape a life that fits them.

About us

We are Thomas and Celina.

We have been together for more than 17 years, married for 10, and are parents to two young children. We were monogamous for our first ten years. For the past seven years, we have explored ethical non-monogamy.

Thomas is an engineer. Celina is a lawyer and certified family-law mediator.

We don't have all the answers. We share our own experiences and perspectives, while staying open to the fact that every person and relationship is unique.
