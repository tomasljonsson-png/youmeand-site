About us

We are Thomas and Celina.

We have been together for more than 15 years, married for 8, and are parents to two young children. For our first ten years, we were monogamous. Over the past five years, we have periodically explored ethical non-monogamy. Our path into alternative relationship structures began with Tantra in 2017.

Thomas is an engineer. Celina is a lawyer and certified family-law mediator.

We don't have all the answers. But we've learned that freedom and security can go hand in hand when you prioritise each other and give your relationship time, energy and care.
